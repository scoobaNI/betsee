# Expense policy

Internal.

- Submit expenses within 30 days with an itemised receipt.
- Train travel: second class; first class only for journeys over 4 hours.
- Hotels: up to 140 EUR per night in Gdansk and Warsaw, 180 EUR elsewhere.
- Meals while travelling: up to 45 EUR per day.
- Anything above 500 EUR needs your budget owner's approval before you spend it.
