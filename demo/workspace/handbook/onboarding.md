# Onboarding at Acme Logistics

Internal. Welcome to the team.

## First week

1. Collect your badge from reception at the Gdansk hub (building B, ground floor).
2. Finish the security awareness course in the learning portal by Friday.
3. Book a 30-minute intro with your team lead and one with the people team.

## Tools

- Laptop: request accessories through the IT service desk, category "Hardware".
- Chat: join #general, #hub-gdansk and your team channel.
- Expenses: see handbook/expense-policy.md.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Hub shifts follow the published rota.
